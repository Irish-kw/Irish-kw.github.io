About

I work on AI systems for security, graph reasoning, and observable agentic software. My recent research focuses on recovering structure from noisy or incomplete evidence, understanding long-running APT campaigns, and building systems that make AI-agent execution behavior inspectable.

Earlier work includes attention-based API locating for malware techniques, a cybersecurity ontology for adversary tactics and techniques, and PM2.5 grid imputation and anomaly prediction. Before that, I worked on numerical simulation of rotary compressors and heat-transfer systems.
